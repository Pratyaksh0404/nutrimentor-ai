# Privacy Policy — NutriMentor AI


## 1. What We Collect

- **Account information**: your name, email, and profile photo, from Google Sign-In.
- **Health profile data you provide**: age, sex, height, weight, fitness goals, health
  conditions/notes (e.g. "high blood pressure," "diabetes"), dietary preferences, and
  dislikes.
- **Usage data**: meals you log, questions you ask the assistant, and diet plans generated
  for you.
- **Technical data**: standard web request logs (IP address, browser type) collected
  automatically by Cloudflare as part of running the service.

## 2. How We Use It

- To personalise diet plans, nutrient analysis, and recommendations to your profile and
  health notes.
- To remember your preferences across sessions.
- To improve the assistant's accuracy over time (see Section 5).

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models beyond what's described in
Section 4, and we do not sell your data.

## 3. What This App Is Not

NutriMentor AI provides general nutrition and wellness information. It is not a
substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a
qualified doctor for medical concerns, especially before making significant dietary
changes if you have a diagnosed health condition.

## 4. Third Parties We Use

- **Google** — for sign-in authentication.
- **Google Gemini API** — some of your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to
  generate responses for questions our deterministic system can't answer directly.
- **Cloudflare** — hosts the app and its data (Workers, D1 database, Vectorize,
  Workers AI). Cloudflare's own privacy policy governs data in transit through their
  infrastructure.

We do not share your health data with advertisers. This app does not run ads.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
- **To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email
  pratyakshagrawal0404@gmail.com.** This is currently a manual process — there is no self-serve
  "delete my account" button in the app. When you request it, we will
  remove your profile, meal logs, health notes, and Google account link
  from our database within 3 days and confirm by email once it's done.

## 6. Data Security

- Access to the app requires Google sign-in.
- Data is stored on Cloudflare's D1 database and KV storage.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your
personal data, and to object to certain processing. 

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be noted with an
updated "Last updated" date above.
